## Reading it
Frame times are 17-19 ms (vsync-bound at 60 Hz) with 0 missed vsyncs and 0 slow-UI-thread frames, but the map is rougher than a
plain scroll here: 22% (pan) and 28% (pinch) of frames count as janky against 5.7% for the control. In this room every seat is
an available outline (no SVG marks); a room full of sold seats draws one SVG cross per seat and was NOT measured.
NOT proven on a real phone. The ADR fallback if a phone shows jank: draw the seat grid as one react-native-svg element.
